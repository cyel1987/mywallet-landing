Get it on your phone

Add Tally to your home screen.

Tally works right in your browser. No app store needed. Add it to your home screen once, and it opens and feels just like any other app on your phone.

📱 iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open app.letstally.app in Safari
  2. Tap the Share icon (the square with an arrow pointing up)
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  4. Tap "Add" in the top corner

🤖 Android (Chrome)

  1. Open app.letstally.app in Chrome
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (top right corner)
  3. Tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app"
  4. Confirm by tapping "Add" or "Install"

📸 Bonus: skip opening the app entirely

On Android, once Tally's installed, just take a photo of a receipt in your regular camera or Photos app, tap Share, and pick Tally: it scans and logs it instantly, no need to open the app first.

On iPhone, a similar shortcut is available through the Shortcuts app. After you sign up, head to Settings inside Tally for the full setup steps.
